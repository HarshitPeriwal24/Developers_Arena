TEST 1: One-Way ANOVA — Regional Sales Differences
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
H₀: Mean Total Sales is equal across all regions
H₁: At least one region has a significantly different mean

Region Breakdown:
  East    : n=19  Mean=$132,613  SD=$77,107
  North   : n=28  Mean=$142,273  SD=$99,202
  South   : n=27  Mean=$138,439  SD=$118,424
  West    : n=26  Mean=$81,689  SD=$87,844

Test Statistic (F): 2.1644
p-value           : 0.0972
Decision          : FAIL TO REJECT H₀
Conclusion        : No significant regional difference found.
Business Impact   : Regional marketing strategy should be adjusted. Underperforming
                    regions need additional investment or targeted campaigns.

TEST 2: Independent Samples t-test — Monthly Charges vs. Churn
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
H₀: E[MonthlyCharges | Churned] ≤ E[MonthlyCharges | Retained]
H₁: Churned customers pay higher monthly charges (one-tailed)

Group Statistics:
  Churned  (n=53): Mean = $129.77,  SD = $46.30
  Retained (n=447): Mean = $111.72, SD = $52.13
  Difference: $+18.05

Test Statistic (t): 2.4102
p-value (one-tailed): 0.0082
Cohen's d (effect): 0.3502 (Small effect size)
Decision           : REJECT H₀ ✓ SIGNIFICANT
Conclusion         : Churned customers pay significantly higher monthly charges.
Business Impact    : High-paying customers are flight risks. Launch proactive retention
                     programs (loyalty discounts, enhanced service) for customers
                     paying above-average monthly fees.

TEST 3: Chi-Square Test of Independence — Contract Type vs. Churn
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
H₀: Contract type and churn are independent (no association)
H₁: Contract type and churn are statistically associated

Contingency Table:
Churn             0   1
Contract               
Month-to-month  135  35
One year        178   8
Two year        134  10

Chi² Statistic : 27.7153
Degrees of Freedom: 2
p-value        : 0.000001
Cramér's V     : 0.2354 (Moderate association)
Decision       : REJECT H₀ ✓ SIGNIFICANT
Conclusion     : Contract type significantly predicts churn behaviour.
Business Impact: Month-to-month customers are significantly more likely to churn.
                 Offer incentives (e.g., 15-20% discount) to upgrade them to
                 annual or two-year contracts.

TEST 4: One-Sample t-test — Is Mean Sales = $100,000?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
H₀: μ(Total_Sales) = $100,000
H₁: μ(Total_Sales) ≠ $100,000 (two-tailed)

Sample Mean : $123,650.48
Benchmark   : $100,000
n           : 100

Test Statistic (t): 2.3612
p-value (two-tailed): 0.0202
Decision           : REJECT H₀ ✓ SIGNIFICANT
Conclusion         : Mean sales differs significantly from the $100,000 benchmark.
Business Impact    : Set realistic quarterly targets based on actual statistical
                     mean with 95% CI bounds rather than arbitrary benchmarks.
